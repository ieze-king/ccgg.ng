# Publishing an article

For anyone writing on the CCGG site. No code, no developer needed.

## Getting in

Go to **https://www.ccgg.ng/studio** and sign in with the Google account
you were invited with. You'll land on a sidebar listing News & Articles,
Media Episodes, Programmes, Resources and Site Settings.

## Writing

**News & Articles → the pencil icon** creates a new article.

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The headline. Sentence case reads better than ALL CAPS. |
| **Slug** | Click Generate. This becomes the web address. |
| **Published on** | Defaults to now. Change it if you're backdating. |
| **Categories** | Up to three. Pick what's true, not everything. |
| **Summary** | One or two sentences. See below, this one matters. |
| **Cover image** | Upload, then fill the Alt text field underneath. |
| **Article** | The body. Headings, bold, links and inline images all work. |

Then **Publish**, top right. It appears on the site within about a minute.
No deploy, nobody else needed.

## The Summary is not optional

It's what shows on the news listing, and it's what appears when someone
shares the link on WhatsApp or Facebook. An article without one posts as a
bare headline with no context, and gets far fewer clicks.

Write it so it stands alone. Say what happened, where, and why it matters.
Keep it under 280 characters.

Good: *Clergy, women and young people gathered at St. Theresa's, Abakpa, on
24 October 2026 as CCGG was officially flagged off. More than 200 bags of
rice were shared on the day.*

Weak: *Read about our recent event.*

## Alt text on images

The box under an uploaded image. One line describing what's in the picture,
for people using screen readers and for anyone whose images fail to load.

Describe the scene, don't caption it. *"Clergy and community members on
stage at the CCGG flag-off in Abakpa"* rather than *"flag-off photo"*.

CCGG publishes disability inclusion as a programme. Worth doing properly.

## Editing something already published

Open it, change it, hit Publish again. Changes are live within a minute.

Sanity keeps revision history, so a bad edit can be rolled back. Deleting a
document is harder to undo. If you're unsure, edit rather than delete.

## House rules

These come from the CCGG editorial code, and the site publishes them at
/standards, so readers can hold us to them.

- Facts before opinions, evidence before claims.
- The same standards apply to every administration.
- Recognise verifiable achievements; acknowledge legitimate shortcomings.
- Correct material errors promptly and visibly, rather than quietly deleting.

## If something looks wrong

Changes take about a minute to show. If it's been longer, hard-refresh the
page first: Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows.
